Why this is vendored
--------------------
A pre-registration must not depend on a shared-drive path that can move or
change underneath it. This mirrors the handling of reference/radlab_snapshot/
and reference/study5_snapshot/.

READ-ONLY. Do not edit anything in this directory. Fixes belong upstream in
TechnicalReference/respkit, after which this snapshot is re-copied and the
fingerprint above is updated.

What this project uses it for
-----------------------------
Task 2 of the pre-registration handoff: a single breath-onset detector shared
by scripts/02_estimate_variance_components.R and the confirmatory analysis, so
that the extracted variance components describe the same signal the
confirmatory analysis sees. R/onsets.R is the adapter; it wraps each device's
25 Hz grid column as a resp_recording and calls one detector with identical
parameters for both devices.

respkit supersedes the direct port of Study 5's find_extrema_prominence that
the handoff's Task 2 originally proposed. Three of the defects it documents
reach this study directly:

  Defect 3   detecting and measuring on one tight band (0.05 to 0.4 Hz)
             symmetrises every breath and biases peak timing late. The fix is
             two filtered copies: detect_band, then snap each extremum onto a
             wider measure_band before measuring. This is why the handoff's
             proposed "port Study 5 wholesale at 0.4 Hz" was rejected.

  Fifth pass flag_breaths() guarded its duration-outlier test with mad > 0, but
             durations are differences of floating-point sample times, so on
             regular breathing the MAD is a residue near 1e-15 rather than
             zero. About half of all breaths were flagged and duration_cv and
             duration_rmssd collapsed to ~1e-15. A paced-breathing protocol
             would have reported zero variability by construction, which would
             have destroyed H5. Fixed upstream.

  Sixth pass duty cycle carries a bias that grows with breathing rate, so any
             manipulation that also changes rate produces a duty-cycle change
             of the same sign for free. H3 manipulates rate by design and EH2
             predicts an inverted U across rate, so this is preregistered as a
             covariate rather than discovered later.

Defect 1 (staged decimation silently returning the wrong sampling rate) does
NOT apply here. R/acq_io.R already factors the decimation exactly and refuses
non-integer factors; its comment cites Study 5's 25.641 Hz as the failure it
was written to avoid.
