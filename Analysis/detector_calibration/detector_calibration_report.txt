CONCLUSION.
The measurement band barely moves the DIFFERENTIAL: paired within seed and
period, 2.0 Hz minus 0.6 Hz is within about 40 ms and its sign depends on
duty cycle. It clearly improves the SINGLE-DEVICE bias, and respkit's own
duty-cycle table puts 2.0 Hz at the knee. ONSET_MEASURE_BAND = 0.05-2.0 Hz.

The finding that matters more than the band:
At duty 0.50 the differential is ~0 at EVERY band. The whole effect is
harmonic distortion from waveform asymmetry, since the accelerometer
amplifies harmonic n by n^2. The BreathBelt pacer is (1-cos)/2, exactly
symmetric, so:
  paced blocks 3 and 4  duty near 0.50, differential offset near zero
  free blocks 2 and 5   duty near 0.43 on respkit's 198-recording archive,
                        giving a differential offset of roughly 135-225 ms
The belt-to-pacer offset is fitted on Block 1, which is PACED and therefore
symmetric, where this offset is zero. It cannot remove the free-breathing
offset. Fitting one there would need between-device timing, which is H2's
estimand and blocked. Hence H2's block-specific tolerance, prereg 5.4.

PART 2. WHY SYNTHETIC CANNOT SET THE PROMINENCE THRESHOLD

A negative result, recorded rather than tuned away. Three attempts to
calibrate min_prominence synthetically returned an identical answer at
every threshold from 0.10 to 0.80:
  1. count against expected returned -2 everywhere (the two edge troughs)
  2. a 1.1 Hz cardiac ripple changed nothing (the 0.6 Hz band removes it)
  3. an in-band 2nd harmonic degraded detection badly but still not by
     threshold, because the extrema it creates are large too

The reason is visible in the prominence distribution. The detection band
smooths broadband noise so thoroughly that synthetic recordings contain
almost no low-prominence extrema, so the whole swept range lies below the
synthetic floor:

                   source n_maxima    min  q25 median  max
 synthetic, noise SD 0.02       25 1.5365 1.92   1.94 2.02
 synthetic, noise SD 0.30       25 1.6021 1.87   1.93 2.09
 synthetic, noise SD 0.80       25 1.3999 1.81   2.01 2.38
 synthetic, noise SD 1.50       27 0.0404 1.69   2.08 2.90

Real signals carry the population of small ripples that a prominence
threshold exists to remove. Same measurement, participant 14542:

        source n_maxima      min   q25 median  max
  14542, accel      453 0.000549 0.221  0.883 71.7
 14542, biopac      340 0.000248 0.691  1.830 10.2

So the threshold is set from Part 3, which is the route Norm approved and
what respkit prescribes: pick it by looking at what gets rejected, not by
its effect on the summary statistics.
